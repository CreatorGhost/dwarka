## Historical recommendation

Use one visual family: Quaternius low-poly CC0 characters, creatures, weapons, and nature. It is coherent, light enough for the browser, and available in glTF/GLB for the important animated assets. Spend $14.99 only on **Universal Animation Library 2 Source** for its bow set; everything else below is free. CC0 assets can ship without attribution or raw-file redistribution restrictions, but keep a provenance file anyway.

The current [Universal Animation Library (UAL1)](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library) is CC0 and supplies free Standard and $9.99 Pro downloads. Its June 2026 build provides root-motion and no-root-motion files. The free Standard GLB was inspected and contains the exact clips `Roll`, `Sword_Attack`, `Hit_Chest`, `Hit_Head`, and `Death01`. [UAL2](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2) is also CC0; its free Standard tier has 43 clips, while the $14.99 Source tier contains the full 130+ set. The official [animation viewer](https://quaternius.com/animviewer.html) exposes the exact Source bow clips `Bow_Notch`, `Bow_Aim_Down`, `Bow_Aim_Neutral`, `Bow_Aim_Up`, `Bow_Shoot`, and `Bow_RapidShoot`; it also includes `Sword_Regular_A/B/C`, `Sword_Regular_Combo`, `Sword_Block`, `Hit_Knockback`, and `Sword_Dash`. Use the no-root-motion exports so Rapier/character-controller movement remains authoritative. If a real lateral dodge matters, UAL1 Pro adds `Dodge_Left`/`Dodge_Right`; otherwise `Roll` is the shippable dodge.

## Integration and production notes

- Convert FBX/OBJ/Blend once in Blender, then ship GLB. Compress geometry with `gltf-transform`/Meshopt and textures to WebP/KTX2. Clone skinned enemies with `SkeletonUtils.clone`; do not duplicate full scene graphs manually.
- UAL1/UAL2 and the current Base Characters share the universal humanoid rig. Apply clips through `AnimationMixer`/Drei `useAnimations`; keep locomotion looping, clamp attack/hit/death once, and crossfade 0.1–0.2 s. Test bone-name compatibility before costume work.
- Target one 1–2K atlas per family, 30–60k visible triangles around the player, instanced environment props, and only one HDR. Lazy-load the next encounter while an illustrated card is displayed.
- The low-poly packs are fantasy-neutral, not culturally authoritative. Adjust palette, cloth, ornaments, bow proportions, and silhouettes; avoid shipping an obviously European knight or Roman chariot unchanged as epic-period India.

## Story A panels and restrictions

Reuse the 32 existing `site/public/story-a/*.webp` images as full-screen transition cards, loading screens, tutorial overlays, and boss-phase stills. This is the highest-value reuse because it preserves the full narrative without building more 3D scenes. Their external license/provenance is not recorded in the repository: they are already part of the project's public review site, but keep them embedded in DWARKA and **do not redistribute them as a standalone art pack** until ownership/source records are confirmed.

All recommended primary downloads are CC0 or MIT and permit a public/commercial demo without attribution. Still ship an `ASSET_CREDITS.md` with creator, URL, license, and download date. Mixamo is only a fallback: Adobe explicitly permits use in games and requires an Adobe ID, but because its FAQ does not grant standalone asset redistribution, distribute only the compiled game experience and necessary embedded runtime files—not a reusable Mixamo download bundle.
